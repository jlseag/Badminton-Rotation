Mabyo host guideGetting started

Step one

Get everyone in

Most hosts already have the night's list in a WhatsApp group. Paste it in rather than typing thirty names one at a time.

  1. Open the Players tab

    The bottom bar is always there. Players is the third icon.

  2. Tap “Paste a list”

    Copy the message from your group chat and paste the whole thing.

  3. Mabyo reads the names

    Numbering, bullets, dots, emoji and blank lines are all stripped out. A line like “1. Budi (lvl 3)” becomes Budi.

  4. Check what it found

    You see the list before anything is added. Anyone already in tonight's list is skipped, so pasting twice is safe.

  5. Add them

    Everyone arrives in one group. Sorting them by level is the next page.

Faster still: once you have hosted a few nights, use Roster history instead. It remembers every player you have ever added, with their level, so a regular night is a few taps.

Mabyo host guideLevels

Step two

Put people in the right level

This is the one thing that decides whether the games are any good. Mabyo builds every match from this order — nothing else.

Advancedclub level, plays everywhere
Upper Intermediatestrong, consistent
Intermediatethe middle of most mabar
Beginnerknows the rules, still learning
Newbiefirst few sessions
New playersnot graded yet
  1. Drag a name to move it

    Use the grip on the right. Drop it inside another level to re-grade, or higher and lower within a level to fine-tune.

  2. The number is their rank

    Position inside the list is what the matchmaker reads. Points won are just the scoreboard — they never change who you play.

  3. Tap a name to rename, re-group or remove
New players is a holding pen, not a skill. Anyone left there is graded as if they were Intermediate, so move them as soon as you have seen them play a game.

Mabyo host guideWho is here

During the night

Who is here, who has gone home

People arrive late and leave early. Mark them away instead of deleting them — the app keeps their games and their level.

  1. Tap “Away” beside a name

    They stop being drawn onto courts straight away. Anyone mid-game finishes the game they are in.

  2. Away rows turn red

    You can see at a glance who is not playing. Tap the button again when they come back.

  3. Nothing is lost

    Games played, wins and level all stay. Somebody who leaves at nine and returns at ten keeps their place in the queue order.

Do not use “Clear tonight's list” for someone going home. That empties the whole night. Away is the button you want.

Mabyo host guideThe game screen

The main screen

Courts and the queue

This is the screen you will spend the night on. Who is on, who is next, and how even each match is.

  1. Waiting, at the top

    The next four to go on. “See all waiting” opens the full queue in order — nobody plays ahead of someone with fewer games.

  2. Each court shows its level

    “Advanced + Upper Intermediate” tells you what kind of game this is. The colour matches the levels playing.

  3. The balance badge

    Very balanced 100% means the two pairs are evenly matched. A lower number means one side is stronger — usually because not enough people of that level are free yet.

  4. Courts fill themselves

    Finish a match and the next four walk on. You do not pick teams.

Mabyo host guideResults

After every game

Save the result in one tap

The fastest part of the night, and the part you do forty times. Picking the winner and drawing the next four is a single action.

  1. Tap the court that finished
  2. Pick the side that won

    Tap either pair. That is the whole entry if you are counting wins.

  3. Tap “Saved · next team”

    The result is stored and the next four are drawn onto the court in the same tap.

  4. Got it wrong? Undo

    A toast appears at the bottom with an Undo button. It puts the four back on the court exactly as they were.

Counting points instead? Settings lets you record scores. First to 21 asks for the loser's score; Combined 21 asks for the winner's. Either way the winner is whoever you tapped.

Mabyo host guideSwapping

When you need to overrule it

Swap anyone onto a court

Someone asks to sit out, or two friends want a game together. You can always override the draw.

  1. Tap a player on court

    Their row lifts and the queue below becomes tappable.

  2. Tap who you want instead

    Any of the four shown, or open See all waiting and take anyone in the queue — including the very last person.

  3. They change places

    The court player goes back to the queue in the other one's position.

  4. Tap the lifted player again to cancel
Swapping is a manual override, so it does not count against anyone's fairness. The queue keeps working normally on the next draw.

Mabyo host guideLeaderboard & history

Keeping score

The leaderboard and the history

Two tabs that answer the two questions people ask you all night: “how many have I played?” and “who won that one?”

  1. Leaderboard

    Wins and games for everyone here tonight. Useful for settling arguments and for spotting who has been waiting too long.

  2. History

    Every match played, newest first, with who was on each side.

  3. Any match can be edited

    Not just the last one. Tap a match in History to change the winner or the score — the leaderboard updates itself.

  4. It survives a reload

    The night is saved on the device as you go. Closing the browser or running out of battery does not lose the session.

Mabyo host guideSettings

Set once, at the start

Settings worth knowing

Most of these you set on your first night and never touch again. Two of them change how the whole evening runs.

  1. Courts

    How many you have booked. Change it mid-session if a court frees up — the queue adjusts on the next draw.

  2. Scoring

    Wins only is one tap per game. Points asks for a score as well. Wins only is what most hosts use.

  3. Level matching

    How strictly Mabyo keeps a court to one level. Looser matching fills courts faster on a quiet night.

  4. Clear tonight's list

    Ends the session and empties the list of who is here — but keeps your roster history, so next week is still quick.

Clearing is not undoable. Mabyo asks first, and the confirmation says how many players will be removed.

Mabyo host guideInstall

Do this once, on your phone

Put Mabyo on your Home Screen

Mabyo runs in the browser, but it is built to be installed. Adding it to your Home Screen takes about fifteen seconds and makes it behave like a normal app. Open mabyo.pages.dev first, then follow your phone.

iPhone & iPad

You must use Safari. Chrome on iPhone cannot install it.

  1. Open mabyo.pages.dev in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button — the square with an arrow pointing up. It is at the bottom of the screen on iPhone, and at the top right on iPad.
  3. Scroll down the grey list of actions.
  4. Tap Add to Home Screen .
  5. Rename it if you like, then tap Add at the top right.
  6. Close Safari. Open Mabyo from your Home Screen from now on.

Android

Chrome, Edge or Samsung Internet all work.

  1. Open mabyo.pages.dev in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu — three dots at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, or Install app if that is what your phone calls it. Both do the same thing.
  4. Tap Install or Add to confirm.
  5. Some phones then ask whether to place the icon — tap Add automatically.
  6. Open Mabyo from your home screen or app drawer from now on.
More screen No address bar and no browser buttons, so you see two or three more rows of the queue on the same phone.
Stays signed in An installed Mabyo keeps your session far longer. A browser tab you have not opened for a week may ask you to sign in again.

Mabyo host guideYour account

Signing in

Your account

Mabyo accounts are issued by the organiser, not signed up for. You should have been sent an email address and a password.

  1. Sign in once per device

    Mabyo remembers you. You should not have to type the password again on that phone.

  2. The session lives on your phone

    Tonight's players, courts and results are stored on the device you are hosting from. Hosting from a different phone starts a separate night.

  3. It works without signal

    Sports halls have bad reception. Mabyo keeps running offline and catches up when the signal returns.

  4. The build number

    Top right of every screen — v69 here. If something looks wrong, send a screenshot; that number says which version you are on.

Do not share your password. One account is meant for one host. If a second person needs to run their own nights, they need their own account.

Mabyo host guideTroubleshooting

On the night

If something goes wrong

Short answers to the things hosts actually run into, in the order they tend to happen.

  1. Someone is on court who should not be

    Tap them, then tap whoever should be playing. See page 7.

  2. I saved the wrong winner

    Hit Undo in the toast straight away, or fix it later from the History tab — any match can be edited, not just the last.

  3. A court is sitting empty

    Usually too few people free at that level. Loosen level matching in Settings, or check how many are marked Away.

  4. Somebody says they have not played in ages

    Open See all waiting. The queue is strictly ordered by games played, so their position is the honest answer.

  5. The page zoomed in when I tapped a box

    Fixed in v69. If it still happens, you are on an old cached version — close all tabs and reopen.

  6. It is asking me to sign in again

    Normal if you have not opened it for a week or so in a browser tab. Installing it to your Home Screen largely stops this — see page 10.

  7. I cleared the list by mistake

    Tonight's players are gone, but Roster history still has everyone with their levels. Re-add them from there.

  8. Nothing is loading

    Mabyo works offline, so this is usually a stale version. Close every Mabyo tab, reopen, and check the build number top right.

Still stuck? Send your organiser a screenshot of the whole screen. The build number in the top right corner is the first thing they will look at.
